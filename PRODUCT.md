# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Entrepreneurs, startups, businesses, venture capital firms, and angel investors evaluating Nolan Innovation's capabilities and shipped work.

## Product Purpose

Establish Nolan Innovation as a credible software studio, present Try Lah clearly, and make direct contact easy. Success means visitors quickly understand what the studio builds, inspect Try Lah, and initiate a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, inventive, assured. Concise and direct rather than promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI-generated landing-page conventions: decorative gradients, repetitive card grids, generic SaaS layouts, excessive copy, gratuitous effects, and animation that compromises performance or usability. Avoid elaborate agency portfolios that require more content than the studio currently has.

## Design Principles

1. Let shipped work establish credibility.
2. Communicate the studio's value in as few words as possible.
3. Use restraint and precision to signal quality.
4. Keep contact paths obvious and frictionless.
5. Add motion only when it improves comprehension or polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion support. The experience must remain usable across screen sizes and input methods.
